St. Mercy Hospital

Closed in 1987. Two floors of dark wards, bathrooms and a morgue. An old woman still walks them.

WASD move · Shift sprint · Mouse look · F flashlight · E take item

She will only show herself three times. After that, she comes for you. Don't run for long, don't make noise (the red bar), and don't stare at her.
